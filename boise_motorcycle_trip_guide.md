# 🏍️ Motorcycle Trip Guide: Mission Viejo, CA to Boise, ID (via Las Vegas)

Comprehensive route planning, campground details, state/national park listings, pricing, and timing recommendations for a Saturday-to-Monday motorcycle journey.

---

## 📊 Trip Overview & Distance Breakdown

* **Total Distance:** ~900 miles total
* **Leg 1 (Saturday Morning):** Mission Viejo, CA → Las Vegas, NV (~275 mi | 4–4.5 hrs)
* **Leg 2 (Saturday Night – Sunday):** Las Vegas, NV → Boise, ID (~625 mi | 9.5–10.5 hrs)
* **Final Destination:** Boise, ID (Arriving Sunday evening for Monday morning meeting)

---

## 📍 Leg 1: Mission Viejo to Las Vegas (Saturday Stopover)

If you need to camp near Las Vegas on Saturday night after your job, or just past town as you head north:

| Park / Campsite | Location / Distance | Nightly Rate | Highlights & Motorcycle Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mojave National Preserve** *(Hole-in-the-Wall Campground)* | Essex/Nipton, CA (~200 mi from MV, off I-15) | **$12 / night** | Mid-elevation high desert (~4,400 ft) with cooler evening temps than Vegas. First-come, first-served. Quiet & great stargazing. |
| **Valley of Fire State Park** *(Atlatl Rock / Arch Rock)* | Moapa Valley, NV (50 mi NE of Vegas) | **$25 / night** *(non-resident)* | Iconic red sandstone formations, great twisty scenic road for bikes. Showers available. Book ahead via ReserveNevada or grab FCFS. |
| **Pahranagat National Wildlife Refuge** *(Upper Pahranagat Lake)* | Alamo, NV (90 mi N of Vegas on US-93) | **FREE** | 15 primitive sites right along the lake. Great stop if you want to get 1.5 hours north of Vegas on Saturday night. Vault toilets, FCFS. |

> ⚠️ **Important Local Note:** *Red Rock Canyon BLM Campground (just west of Vegas) is **closed annually from June 1 to August 31** due to extreme desert heat.*

## 🗺️ Leg 2: Las Vegas to Boise (Saturday Night / Sunday)

There are 3 main routes from Las Vegas to Boise. **US-93** or **US-95** are the most scenic for motorcycles.

### Option A: US-93 North (The "Great Basin Highway" – Highly Recommended for Motorcycles)
*Scenic high-desert route, cool mountain elevations, excellent state parks, low traffic.*

| Park / Campsite | Location | Nightly Rate | Highlights & Motorcycle Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cathedral Gorge State Park** | Panaca, NV (165 mi N of Vegas) | **$20 / night** *(non-resident)* | Dramatic eroded clay cathedral spires. Clean shade structures, flush toilets, and hot showers. Very rider-friendly. |
| **Cave Lake State Park** | Ely, NV (245 mi N of Vegas) | **$20 / night** *(non-resident)* | Mountain lake at 7,300 ft elevation. Pine trees, cool night air, excellent relief from desert heat. Showers available. |
| **City of Rocks National Reserve** | Almo, ID (Off US-93/I-84 near ID border) | **~$12–$21 / night** + $7 vehicle fee | World-class granite monoliths and sweeping mountain valley views. High-grade scenery for a motorcycle stop. Reserve online. |
| **Bruneau Dunes State Park** | Bruneau, ID (60 mi S of Boise) | **~$24–$35 / night** + $7 vehicle fee | North America's tallest single-structure sand dune. Small lake, observatory, shade trees. Great spot for Sunday night. |

---

### Option B: US-95 North (Western Nevada / Remote Desert Route)
*Direct, wide-open high desert riding through Nevada into Eastern Oregon/Idaho.*

| Park / Campsite | Location | Nightly Rate | Highlights & Motorcycle Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Millers Rest BLM Area** | Tonopah, NV (215 mi NW of Vegas) | **FREE** | Basic BLM rest stopover with picnic tables and shade. Convenient if pushing northwest past Vegas on Saturday evening. |
| **Water Canyon Recreation Area (BLM)** | Winnemucca, NV (380 mi N of Vegas) | **FREE** | Tucked into a mountain canyon with shade trees, running creek, and fire rings. A hidden gem free site for campers. |
| **Rye Patch State Recreation Area** | Lovelock, NV (Near I-80 / US-95) | **$20 / night** *(non-resident)* | Reservoir campsite on the Humboldt River. Mature trees, shade, and showers. |
| **Succor Creek State Natural Area** | Malheur County, OR (Off US-95 near ID border) | **FREE** | Remote, dramatic rock canyon walls. Primitive camping with vault toilets. Peaceful and quiet. |
| **Farewell Bend State Park** | Huntington, OR (I-84/US-95 near Snake River) | **~$21–$24 / night** *(tent site)* | Grassy shaded campsites along the Snake River on the Oregon/Idaho border. Hot showers. |

### Option C: I-15 N to I-84 W (Interstate Route through Utah & Idaho)
*Fastest route with frequent gas stations, food, and amenities.*

| Park / Campsite | Location | Nightly Rate | Highlights & Motorcycle Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red Cliffs Recreation Area (BLM)** | St. George, UT (120 mi NE of Vegas) | **$15–$20 / night** | Red rock canyon backdrop, shady streamside sites. FCFS / Recreation.gov. |
| **Massacre Rocks State Park** | American Falls, ID (460 mi N of Vegas on I-84) | **~$24–$36 / night** + $7 fee | Shaded riverfront sites along the Snake River. Rich history, clean restrooms & showers. |
| **Lake Walcott State Park** | Rupert, ID (500 mi N of Vegas) | **~$24–$32 / night** + $7 fee | Quiet park with lush green grass lawns right on the reservoir. |
| **Three Island Crossing State Park** | Glenns Ferry, ID (70 mi SE of Boise on I-84) | **~$24–$36 / night** + $7 fee | Historical Oregon Trail river crossing. Grassy, well-shaded tent sites, clean showers. |

## 🏙️ Destination Camping: Right Near Boise (Sunday Night)

If you want to arrive near Boise on Sunday evening so you're clean, rested, and ready for your Monday business meeting:

1. **Shafer Butte Campground (Boise National Forest)** – *~16 miles N of downtown Boise*
   * **Price:** **$10 / night**
   * **Vibe:** Tent-only mountain campground at 7,000 ft elevation up Bogus Basin Road. 
   * **Why it's great for bikes:** Cool mountain air for sleeping, incredible sunset views over the valley, and only a 35-minute winding ride down into your Monday meeting in downtown Boise.
2. **Celebration Park** – *Melba, ID (~35 miles S of Boise)*
   * **Price:** **~$10–$15 / night**
   * **Vibe:** Quiet primitive camping along the Snake River surrounded by basalt cliffs and petroglyphs.
3. **Boise Riverside RV Park / KOA** – *Garden City / Boise Metro*
   * **Price:** **~$35–$50 / night** *(tent site)*
   * **Vibe:** Urban campsite right on the Boise River Greenbelt if you prefer hot showers, laundry, and fast access to city amenities before your meeting.

## ⏱️ Recommended Itinerary

* **Saturday Morning:** Ride from Mission Viejo to Las Vegas via I-15 N (~4 hrs). Complete your job in Vegas.
* **Saturday Afternoon / Evening:** Ride 1.5 to 2.5 hours north out of Vegas heat up US-93 N. Camp at **Pahranagat NWR (Free)** or **Cathedral Gorge State Park ($20)**.
* **Sunday:** Ride US-93 N through Ely and Wells, connecting to I-84 W into Idaho. Camp Sunday night at **Bruneau Dunes State Park ($24–$35)** or up at **Shafer Butte ($10)** near Boise.
* **Monday Morning:** Roll down into Boise fresh for your meeting!

---

## 💡 Motorcycle Pro-Tips for this Route

* **Fuel Range:** If taking US-95 or US-93 through Nevada, gas stations can be **70–100 miles apart** (e.g., between Tonopah & Winnemucca, or Alamo & Ely). Always fill up when you hit a town!
* **Summer Heat:** Mojave Desert and Vegas valley temperatures regularly exceed 100°F. Plan your heavy riding early morning or late afternoon, and stay hydrated.
